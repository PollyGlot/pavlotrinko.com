# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with MDX content collections, served from Cloudflare Workers (static assets). Deploy through CI on the self-hosted runner, then `wrangler deploy`. Domain: `pavlotrinko.com` (to be bought on Cloudflare Registrar).

## Users

- **Builders and developers** who also build with AI agents. They come for the notes and the method.
- **Users of Pavlo's apps** (mobile and desktop) who discover a product and want to know who makes it.
- **Clients and recruiters** who evaluate Pavlo for a mission, a role or a collaboration.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Pavlo Trinko. It holds three kinds of content: a showcase of products and projects, a blog for long posts, and short notes (small tips, in the spirit of Wes Bos's "hot tips"). Success: a visitor gets in touch with Pavlo.

## Positioning

Pavlo is a **Product AI Builder**: he builds products, mostly mobile and desktop apps, by steering AI agents, and keeps the product decisions and the review for himself. The site is both the proof of that way of working and the place where he documents it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual, French and English, from day one: every page and every post can exist in both languages.
- Static site, with zero JavaScript by default. Interactive pieces are islands, added only when a page needs one.
- Publishing a note means writing one MDX file in Git and pushing it. There is no CMS.
- Planned: RSS feed, generated Open Graph images, sitemap, code highlighting.
- Open decision: which products the showcase lists. It ships first as a mockup with placeholder entries, and the real list comes later.

## Brand Commitments

- Identity: **Pavlo Trinko** (real name, never "Paul"), signed **PollyGlot**, his handle on GitHub and social networks.
- Existing asset: the PollyGlot avatar used on GitHub and social networks.

## Evidence on Hand

- The PollyGlot avatar (GitHub profile).
- No testimonials, clients, metrics or press yet: do not invent any.
- The product list is still to be confirmed: placeholder entries must be labelled as placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work, don't claim it: real projects, real notes, real process.
2. Contact is always one step away, on every page.
3. Writing comes first: posts and notes must be a pleasure to read in both languages.
4. Light by default: content loads fast everywhere, with no unnecessary JavaScript.
